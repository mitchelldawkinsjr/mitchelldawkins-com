In September I wrote **[Little Sprouts: Play That Teaches](/blog/little-sprouts-play-and-learn)**. That post covers the basics: three learners, four ways to play, and a Parent corner for levels and sight words. Start there if you are new.

This post covers what changed since then. The short version: more games, more reasons to keep playing, and more devices it runs on. Still no account. Still free.

## See it play

Here is a real round of **Canyon Pods**. You race three garden buddies through a desert canyon. At each glowing gate the race pauses and asks a question. A right answer charges a boost and a shield.

<img src="/images/projects/little-sprouts/canyon-pods-play.gif" alt="Canyon Pods gameplay: countdown, racing through the canyon, answering a counting question at a checkpoint, then boosting with a shield" />

The toy is a racer. The question is the same counting question from the learner's math band. That is still the whole idea.

## A new home screen

The shelf grew from a dozen cards to **66 games**, so the home screen was rebuilt. Games are grouped into shelves: Featured, New, Calm & Create, Quick Play, Power Play, Run, Jump & Dodge, Drive, Fly & Aim, and Words & Numbers. Every card shows a real picture from the game.

<img src="/images/projects/little-sprouts/v2-home.jpg" alt="The new Little Sprouts home screen with shelf tabs and Featured Games cards" />

Each game now opens in **Focus** view. The game fills the screen and the learning prompt stays on top. There is a fullscreen button for tablets.

## Stars now buy something

Before, stars were just a count. Now the **Star shop** turns them into gear for your garden buddy: 42 items, including whole outfits. The buddy also became the player in every game that has a character, so the hat you bought shows up in the race, the maze, and the block world.

<img src="/images/projects/little-sprouts/v2-shop.jpg" alt="Star shop with the garden fox in the dressing room and the next treasure to earn" />

## New games

A few highlights from the new shelf:

- **Boss chase** and **Ink Trail**: build your own boss (caterpillar, spider, shark, squid, gator, octopus, or a mix), then get away from it.
- **Block meadow**: walk a big block world, find gift boxes, answer, and build with the blocks you win.
- **Sketchbook Mega Ramp**: drive down the ramp, then tour the playground and light beacons.
- **Paint Rivals**: a one-on-one paint duel in the new Power Play shelf.
- **Cabin camp**, **Skybound climb**, **Garden gulp**, **Marble box**, **Grass maze**, **Pebble roll**, **Pinecone Trail**, **Moonlight Swing**, and more.
- **Quick Math** and **Memory verse** for older kids. Memory verse reads a passage once, then the child reads it back.

Some of the big 3D games are heavy. They stay off until a grown-up turns them on in Parent corner. A **Lite mode** also scales heavy games down on weak devices, so an older tablet still gets a smooth round.

## Play with a friend

**Friend rooms** let two kids join the same game. You can pair with a QR code or a short sound "chirp", and it prefers your home Wi-Fi when it can. Paint Sphere, Cabin camp, Block meadow, Inkwave, and several others support it. When a friend joins, the game waits so nobody gets stacked questions.

## Any controller, any screen

- **Game controllers** work in every game, with one shared controller layout.
- **Touch controls** were added for phones and tablets, including a floating joystick that appears where your thumb lands.
- **Tilt steering** works in the racing games.

## Offline, and on a Fire tablet

Little Sprouts is now an installable web app that works **offline**. A parent can save the full pack once, and each file is stored a single time.

My kids use Amazon Fire tablets, so there is now a **Fire tablet app** too. It updates itself over Wi-Fi. Each update is checked against a published checksum before it installs.

## Read-aloud got sturdier

The spoken question is now louder than the game, and game sound dips while it talks. Each question is read when the game screen opens, not before. Common lines are pre-recorded so they play right away, and iPhone read-aloud recovers when Safari interrupts it.

## What did not change

Progress still stays in the browser. Reach still never records or uploads video. Every borrowed piece is still credited on the site under **Credits** and in the [README](https://github.com/mitchelldawkinsjr/little-sprouts#credits). And the toy still changes while the question stays the same.
