Film Room Downloader saves game video from your Hudl tab, using the login you already have, so you can review it offline. Install it from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/film-room-downloader/kjnadjpdehgoghbbflbbffnpeoonhkbe).

Open a video you have access to, agree to the one-time notice in the extension, then choose Download. Chrome saves the file into your Downloads folder. A small sidecar file stores the play labels shown on the page — play number, down, distance, formation, result, quarter, and similar — so you can tell the clips apart.

Download All saves every video detected on that tab, two at a time.

Open Film Room Review opens a local viewer for those saved files. You can play, pause, slow down, step frame by frame, and draw notes that stay tied to the moment you drew them. Notes stay on your computer.

The extension uses your existing login. It never asks for your password. It only runs on hudl.com. Nothing is sent to the developer.

The field story is [Film Room: Review When the Connection Drops](/blog/film-room-offline-review). How the player and the extension fit together is on the [Offline Film Room + Hudl Downloader](/projects/film-room) project page.
